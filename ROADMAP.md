# Semantic Reasoning Project Roadmap

**Canonical version:** 1.0.0
**Current stage:** S0 only

Stages are authorization boundaries, not release labels. Completion of one
stage must be evidenced before the next begins. No future stage below is
claimed complete.

## S0 — Canonical Foundation Freeze

Create the Constitution, falsifiable hypotheses, conceptual substrate and
reasoning architecture, experiment controls, inherited-component inventory,
recursive-improvement quarantine, project state, and canonical hash manifest.
No semantic reasoner is implemented.

**Exit gate:** All S0 validation checks pass; canonical hashes verify; inherited
mutation paths are quarantined; baseline tests pass; the baseline is committed.

## SEM-0 — Minimal Autonomous Concept Emergence

Build a small closed-world executable substrate and the minimum experimental
harness. Attempt to prove or falsify emergence of at least one genuinely
reusable concept beyond primitive-only, solution-cache, and structural-macro
baselines.

**Exit evidence:** Fresh blind transfer, counterfactual consistency, causal
ablation, provenance, benefit, and regression gates under the frozen protocol.

## SEM-1 — Recursive Concept Ladder

Allow validated promoted concepts to become inputs for later concept discovery.

**Goal:** Demonstrate a multi-generation abstraction lineage whose higher
generation causally depends on a lower autonomously promoted generation.

## SEM-2 — Adaptive Reasoning Complexity

Introduce tasks requiring materially different reasoning graph shapes and
substantially varying depth, width, branches, working sets, decomposition, and
recombination.

**Goal:** Demonstrate resource-bounded adaptive allocation rather than fixed
reasoning depth.

## SEM-3 — Active Experiment Selection

Introduce bounded active-inference or information-gain-driven selection of
experiments and counterfactual probes.

**Goal:** Improve evidence efficiency without answer access, uncontrolled
exploration, or weakened verification.

## SEM-4 — Mathematics Domain

Provide minimal axioms and definitions but no high-level target formulas,
algorithms, or proofs.

**Goal:** Test autonomous derivation, reusable abstraction, and transfer in a
formal domain.

## SEM-5 — Programming Domain Expansion

Move from a toy executable DSL toward real programming abstractions while
preserving sandboxing, provenance, and solution-leakage controls.

**Goal:** Discover and validate reusable executable programming concepts, not
merely synthesize seen implementations.

## SEM-6 — Definition-Only Knowledge Foraging

Introduce controlled retrieval of external definitions, API contracts, and
primitive documentation. Retrieval of active-problem solutions remains
forbidden.

**Goal:** Demonstrate auditable integration of definitions without solution
contamination.

## SEM-7 — Language–Concept Adapter

Attach multilingual lexical forms to already existing concepts and test
concept operation before and after naming.

**Goal:** Demonstrate concepts without names, optional lexical grounding, and
alias changes that do not mutate semantics.

## SEM-8 — Cross-Domain Concept Transfer

Test whether concepts learned in one domain assist another through structural
analogy without target solutions.

**Goal:** Establish verified cross-domain causal transfer rather than shared
labels or benchmark overlap.

## SEM-9 — Recursive Self-Application Sandbox

Only at this stage may discovered concepts propose changes to reasoning
machinery. All proposals remain sandboxed, causally compared, regression
checked, independently verified, rollback capable, and explicitly gated before
any application.

**Goal:** Test H8 without granting unrestricted source mutation.

## SEM-10 — Scaling Campaign

Evaluate large concept counts, sparse activation, accelerated exploration, and
parallel experiments under complete cost and correctness accounting.

**Goal:** Test scaling hypotheses while preserving semantic evidence,
determinism where required, and fail-closed behavior.

## Advancement rule

The next stage may refine implementation details but may not silently change
constitutional meanings, retroactively relabel earlier artifacts, or treat a
later capability as evidence for a failed earlier gate. Any constitutional
change follows the amendment process and triggers review of affected results.
