# Contextual natural-conversation success profile

This addendum does not modify or relax the frozen R64 completion contract. It records the user's narrower product objective:

> B_Core does not need to exceed GPT. Success means that it can understand the user's intent and the dialogue context and produce a natural response.

## Acceptance rule

The bounded product profile passes when all of the following hold:

1. structural intent/context suites pass without semantic or execution authority;
2. the frozen 20-case diagnostic and 100-case metamorphic suite both pass completely;
3. long-horizon discourse, pragmatic transfer, and six-axis integration pass completely;
4. the raw natural-expression contract score is at least 85%;
5. product and portable-package Rust unit tests pass;
6. external LLM, local teacher, network, and recursive source-mutation counts remain zero;
7. generation stages do not overwrite one another.

## Non-claim

This result does not claim GPT parity, unrestricted open-domain language competence, or human equivalence. It establishes the requested engineering threshold for the controlled Korean/English Language Cortex and provides a stable baseline for real-user dialogue evaluation.

The sealed 12-case transfer suite remains unopened under the original frozen contract.
